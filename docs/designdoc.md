# Reassembled - Lightsaber Item Overhaul

Reassembled is a mod that alters how lightsabers are acquired. 

The first change is the option to receive a lightsaber immediately after leaving Telos. This option is implemented through a diagetic dialogue option that can be bypassed if not desired for the given playthrough. 

The second change is the option to find lightsabers and their parts from vendors and loot containers, allowing you to craft lightsabers throughout the game. The frequency and extent are configured upon installation.

The third change adds the option to create a Short Lightsaber when making a lightsaber from Bao-Dur. This change will always be active as it's a simple QoL improvement.

## Change 1: Bypassing the *Crafting a Lightsaber* quest

This is implemented by adding a dialogue option to T3 following the viewing of your exile. He will beep and you'll be able to respond with two options:

> 1. These are lightsaber parts. How did you get these?
> 2. That's only junk, T3.

If you choose the first option, the quest will progress to the point that you can craft the lightsaber. When Bao-Dur talks to you about building a lightsaber, you will be given an option that will let you build a lightsaber right away:

> 1. T3 "found" some parts from Telos. Are these what you're talking about?
> 2. <something ruder and more darkside>

## Change 2: Lightsabers found in Loot

Inspired by Snigaroo's request, this adds lightsabers and the three fixtures to the loot pool. When you have found all three parts, you can go to a workbench and make a new lightsaber. Lightsabers and their parts will only be added to the loot pool once *Crafting a Lightsaber* is complete.

There are several options defining frequency of the items in loot.

Full lightsabers: frequently, infrequently, never
Lightsaber parts: frequently, infrequently, never
Vendors carry lightsaber parts: always, frequently, infrequently, never
Certain vendors carry lightsabers: yes, no

Certain vendors include <!spoiler>Daaraala, Kex, (someone on Nar Shaddaa)</!spoiler>.

## Change 3: Short Lightsaber

Simply adds an option to the [What kind of lightsaber do you want to create?] dialogue that adds a Short Lightsaber to your inventory.
